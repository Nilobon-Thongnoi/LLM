QUESTION A: 10 POINTS
---------------------------------

A1. Appropriate Data Structures (3 points)

3: Defines suitable structures for diseases, symptoms and their relationships, and explains their use clearly.
2: Selects suitable structures but omits some details or explanations.
1: Identifies a relevant structure without showing adequately how data is organized.
0: No valid or relevant data structure.

A2. Search Algorithm (4 points)

4: Explains a logically valid process to match patient symptoms with diseases, including how matches are counted or evaluated.
3: The process is mostly correct but missing minor details.
2: The main matching idea is reasonable but several steps are unclear.
1: Gives only a vague search idea with little usable logic.
0: No valid search process.

A3. Top 3 Ranking (3 points)

3: Defines a ranking method consistent with the chosen matching approach and explains selecting the top 3 diseases.
2: Ranking is mostly correct but omits an important detail.
1: Mentions returning 3 diseases without a clear selection rule.
0: Does not provide a usable ranking or selection method.

---------------------------------
QUESTION B: 10 POINTS
---------------------------------

B1. Variables (2 points)

2: Defines N and M correctly according to the question.
1: Defines only one variable correctly or gives incomplete definitions.
0: Incorrect or missing definitions.

B2. Time Complexity Analysis (4 points)

4: Analyzes the relevant search operations, loops and data structure costs correctly for the proposed algorithm.
3: Mostly correct with a minor omission.
2: Partially correct reasoning but misses important operations.
1: Shows minimal relevant analysis.
0: No valid analysis.

B3. Final Big-O and Explanation (4 points)

4: States a correct worst-case bound, justified by the selected algorithm and its assumptions.
3: Correct bound with incomplete justification.
2: A partially justified bound with a significant error or missing cost.
1: Gives an unsupported or mostly incorrect bound with limited relevant reasoning.
0: No valid result or explanation.

Important:
O(N*M) is not the only acceptable answer.
The result depends on the algorithm, preprocessing and data structure assumptions.
Do not assume average-case hash lookup is worst-case O(1) without stating the assumption or using an appropriate worst-case guarantee.

---------------------------------
QUESTION C: 5 POINTS
---------------------------------

C1. First Limitation and Improvement (2 points)

2: Identifies a relevant practical limitation and a suitable improvement.
1: Identifies the limitation but provides an incomplete or weak improvement, or vice versa with partial relevance.
0: No relevant limitation and improvement.

C2. Second Limitation and Improvement (2 points)

2: Identifies a different relevant limitation and a suitable improvement.
1: Partially correct limitation or improvement.
0: Missing or incorrect.

C3. Justification (1 point)

1: Explains logically how the proposed improvements address the limitations.
0: No meaningful explanation.
