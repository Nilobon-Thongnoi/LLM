========================================
R1: TABLES / ENTITIES (10 POINTS)
========================================

OFFICIAL CRITERION:
จำนวน Table ที่เกี่ยวข้องครบถ้วน (10 คะแนน)

REFERENCE DESIGN:
Department
Employee
Employee Type
Office
Department-Office (junction table)

PROPOSED PARTIAL CREDIT:

2 points: Department is represented.
2 points: Employee is represented.
2 points: Employee Type is represented.
2 points: Office is represented.
2 points: Department-Office many-to-many
          relationship is resolved appropriately.

MAXIMUM: 10

IMPORTANT:
A valid alternative design may represent employee
types using a field instead of a separate table.

A valid alternative may also use a different
relationship design if its constraints satisfy
the requirement.

Do not automatically deduct points only because
a design has fewer tables than the reference.

========================================
R2: FIELDS AND DATA TYPES (10 POINTS)
========================================

OFFICIAL CRITERION:
รายละเอียด ชื่อ fields และ data types ครบถ้วน
อย่างน้อยเท่ากับที่จำเป็นต้องมี (10 คะแนน)

PROPOSED PARTIAL CREDIT:

2 points:
Department has meaningful identifying fields.

2 points:
Employee has meaningful identifying fields.

2 points:
Employee type or employment classification
is represented correctly.

2 points:
Office has meaningful identifying fields,
including a way to identify the head office.

2 points:
Primary keys, foreign keys and data types
are appropriate for the design.

MAXIMUM: 10

GRADING NOTES:
- Accept equivalent field names.
- Accept reasonable compatible SQL data types.
- Do not require one exact VARCHAR length.
- Do not penalize cosmetic naming differences.
- Consider PK/FK placement and validity.
- Avoid penalizing the same missing field repeatedly.

========================================
R3: RELATIONSHIPS AND CARDINALITY (8 POINTS)
========================================

OFFICIAL CRITERION:
Relationships ระหว่าง Table ครบถ้วน
และระบุ Cardinality ถูกต้อง (8 คะแนน)

PROPOSED PARTIAL CREDIT:

2 points:
Department and Employee relationship
is correctly represented.

2 points:
Employee and Employee Type relationship
or equivalent classification is valid.

2 points:
Department and Office relationship
is correctly represented.

2 points:
Cardinality and relationship implementation
are consistent with the proposed database design.

MAXIMUM: 8

GRADING NOTES:
- Department to Employee is normally 1:M.
- Employee Type to Employee is normally 1:M
  when Employee Type is a separate table.
- Department to Office can be M:N through
  a junction table in the reference design.
- Evaluate alternative representations by
  their actual semantics.
- Do not give full credit for a relationship
  that contradicts the requirements.

========================================
R4: ADDITIONAL USEFUL FIELDS (2 POINTS)
========================================

OFFICIAL CRITERION:
Fields เพิ่มเติมที่น่าสนใจ หรือเพิ่มเติมจาก
ที่โจทย์ระบุอย่างเหมาะสม (2 คะแนน)

PROPOSED PARTIAL CREDIT:

2 points:
Adds useful, relevant fields and explains or
demonstrates their purpose.

1 point:
Adds a relevant field, but its use or
appropriateness is limited or unclear.

0 points:
No meaningful additional fields.

EXAMPLES:
- employee_email
- hire_date
- office_phone
- department_description

Do not award points for unrelated fields.

========================================
GENERAL AI GRADING RULES
========================================

1. Evaluate the ER Diagram requirements.
2. Award points independently per criterion.
3. Support partial credit.
4. Accept alternative valid designs.
5. Do not require exact matching with the
   reference diagram.
6. Do not require exact naming.
7. Validate the semantics of relationships.
8. Check cardinality and PK/FK consistency.
9. Consider whether the data model can
   represent the business requirements.
10. Do not reward unrelated extra tables.
11. Do not double-penalize one missing element.
12. Give reasons for all awarded points.
13. If the diagram cannot be parsed,
    flag for review instead of inventing data.
14. If the diagram is empty, score zero.
15. Never exceed the maximum score.
