TABLE 5: Department_Office

Fields:

Purpose:
Represents the many-to-many relationship
between departments and offices.

========================================
REFERENCE RELATIONSHIPS
========================================

1. Department -> Employee

Cardinality:
1:M

One department can contain many employees.
Each employee belongs to one department.

Implementation:
Employee.dept_id references Department.dept_id.

2. Employee_Type -> Employee

Cardinality:
1:M

One employment type can apply to many employees.
Each employee has one employment type.

Implementation:
Employee.emp_type_id references
Employee_Type.emp_type_id.

3. Department <-> Office

Cardinality:
M:N

A department can operate in multiple offices.
An office can accommodate multiple departments.

Implementation:
Department_Office is used as a junction table.

Department -> Department_Office = 1:M
Office -> Department_Office = 1:M
